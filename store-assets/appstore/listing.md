## English (U.S.)

### Name
Lexistencehub

### Subtitle
English words & grammar

### Promotional text
English at your level: vocabulary with pictures, A1–C1 grammar tests, crosswords, games and AI speaking practice. Start for free.

### Keywords
english,vocabulary,grammar,ielts,yds,ydt,lgs,turkish,exam,crossword,flashcards,speaking,quiz,learn

### Description
Lexistencehub is an English learning app made for Turkish-speaking students. It brings vocabulary, grammar, listening, writing and speaking practice together in one place and shapes everything around your level and your goal.

FIND YOUR LEVEL, START THERE
A short placement test sets your level from A1 to C2. Questions get harder or easier as you answer, so you start exactly where you should.

CONTENT FOR YOUR GOAL
• General English
• LGS, unit by unit
• YDT, YDS, YÖKDİL and IELTS vocabulary
• Phrasal verbs, irregular verbs, adjectives, adverbs, nouns and connectors
• Grammar topics and tests

SEVEN WAYS TO STUDY
• Listening: hear the word, write it correctly
• Writing: see the Turkish meaning, write the English word
• Visual Learning: word cards with pictures
• Matching: pair words with their Turkish meanings
• Stories: short stories built around your words
• Conversations: see words in real dialogues
• AI Lex: an AI English coach that answers your questions and corrects mistakes kindly

PLAY WHILE YOU LEARN
Crosswords, Wordlock, The A–Z, What Am I?, Word Build, Unbroken, Golden Match, The Clue, Odd One, Word Path and Grammar Duel turn vocabulary and grammar into games. In Crosswords you solve English words from Turkish clues: 150 levels in 3 difficulties, with jokers.

TALK WITH AI
AI Speaking is a real-time voice conversation in English: speak, listen and get an answer at once.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Learning time, daily streak and study by method are in your statistics. Performance analysis shows your strengths and weak spots.

A WORD EVERY DAY
Wordrobe brings a new word every day with its meaning and an example.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The free plan includes 10 words, 3 games, 1 grammar and 1 listening activity every day. The yearly Lexistencehub Premium subscription unlocks unlimited words, games, grammar and listening, AI Lex chats, AI Speaking, personalized learning and performance analysis.

Subscription information: Lexistencehub Premium is a 1-year auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the renewal is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage the subscription and turn off auto-renewal in your App Store account settings after purchase.

Your account keeps your progress safe, so you continue where you left off on every device.
