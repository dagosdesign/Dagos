## English (en-US) — çeviri olarak eklenecek

### App name
Lexistencehub

### Short description
Learn English at your level with words, grammar, games and AI Lex.

### Full description
Lexistencehub is an English learning app made for Turkish-speaking students. It brings vocabulary, grammar, listening, writing and speaking practice together in one place and shapes everything around your level and your goal.

FIND YOUR LEVEL, START THERE
A short placement test sets your level from A1 to C2. Questions get harder or easier as you answer, so you start exactly where you should.

CONTENT FOR YOUR GOAL
• General English
• LGS, unit by unit
• YDT, YDS, YÖKDİL and IELTS vocabulary
• Phrasal verbs, irregular verbs, adjectives, adverbs, nouns and connectors
• Grammar topics and tests

SEVEN WAYS TO STUDY
• Listening: hear the word, write it correctly
• Writing: see the Turkish meaning, write the English word
• Visual Learning: word cards with pictures
• Matching: pair ten words with their Turkish meanings
• Stories: short stories built around your target words
• Conversations: see words in real dialogues
• AI Lex: an AI-powered English coach that answers your questions and gently corrects your mistakes

PLAY WHILE YOU LEARN
Reinforce vocabulary and grammar with Wordlock, The A–Z, What Am I?, Word Build, Unbroken, Golden Match, The Clue, Odd One, Word Path and Grammar Duel. Grammar Duel takes you from A1 to C1 across 50 levels.

TALK WITH AI
AI Speaking is a real-time voice conversation in English: speak, listen and get replies instantly.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Learning time, daily streak, active days and time per method are all in your statistics. Performance Analysis shows your strengths and weak spots.

A NEW WORD EVERY DAY
Wordrobe brings a new word every day, with its meaning and an example sentence.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The Free plan includes 10 words, 3 games, 1 grammar and 1 listening activity every day. A Premium subscription unlocks unlimited words, games, grammar and listening, AI Lex conversations, AI Speaking, personalised learning and Performance Analysis. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled; you can cancel any time in your Google Play subscription settings.

Your account keeps your progress safe, so you can continue on any device.
